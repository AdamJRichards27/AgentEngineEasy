## 1. Two kinds of complaint — and where each one goes

It matters what your complaint is actually about:

- **A complaint about the Platform itself** — a bug, a privacy concern, how a request was handled,
  an AI-assisted suggestion, or the conduct of [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] staff — goes to [LEGAL ENTITY
  NAME] using the process below.
- **A complaint about the Brokerage's real estate services** — for example, how a transaction was
  conducted, an agent's conduct, or a trust account concern — is a matter for the Brokerage itself,
  and, if unresolved, the applicable Queensland regulator (for example, the Office of Fair Trading
  Queensland, under the real estate agent licensing framework — **the correct current regulator
  and pathway must be confirmed by a qualified Australian lawyer** before this document is
  finalised). The Platform is a tool the Brokerage uses; using the Platform does not change who is
  responsible for the Brokerage's own conduct.

If you're not sure which kind your complaint is, send it to [COMPLAINTS CONTACT EMAIL] and we will
tell you if it needs to be redirected to the Brokerage or a regulator instead.

## 2. How to make a complaint about the Platform

Email [COMPLAINTS CONTACT EMAIL] with: what happened, when, who was involved (roles, not
necessarily names), and what outcome you're looking for.

## 3. What happens next

1. **Acknowledgement.** We aim to acknowledge receipt within [PERIOD, e.g. 3 business days].
2. **Investigation.** We will look into what happened, which may include reviewing system logs and
   speaking with relevant staff.
3. **Response.** We aim to provide a substantive response within [PERIOD, e.g. 30 days]. If it will
   take longer, we will tell you why and give a revised timeframe.
4. **Outcome.** We will explain what we found and, where appropriate, what we are doing about it
   (for example, a product fix, a process change, or a correction to a record).

## 4. Privacy complaints specifically

If your complaint is that your personal information was mishandled, see also the Pilot Privacy
Policy and the Data Access, Correction, and Deletion Request Process. If you are not satisfied with
our response to a privacy complaint, you may be able to take it further to the Office of the
Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) — **to be confirmed** as the correct external body once
this pilot's privacy compliance basis has been properly reviewed.

## 5. No cost to complain

There is no charge for making a complaint, and doing so will not affect your access to the Platform
except where a complaint itself concerns misuse of an account (in which case ordinary account
security processes may apply, separately from the complaint being heard fairly).

## 6. Record-keeping

Complaints and their outcomes are logged so patterns can be identified across the pilot. This
record is handled per the Pilot Privacy Policy and Data Retention Schedule.
