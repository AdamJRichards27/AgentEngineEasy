legally binding contract. It must be reviewed by a qualified Australian lawyer, and adapted to

## 1. Purpose and scope

The Provider will make the Agent Engine platform available to the Brokerage for a single controlled
pilot, for the purpose of evaluating the Platform's workflow, transaction-tracking, and AI-assisted
features in a real operating environment, under closely bounded conditions.

## 3. Commercial terms

The pilot is provided free of charge, or invoiced manually by the Provider to the Brokerage, as
separately agreed. All amounts, if any, are in Australian dollars (AUD), with GST (assumed at the
standard 10% rate, pending confirmation of the Provider's GST registration status) applied where
applicable. **No automated subscription billing, direct debit, or automated success-fee/commission
payment is authorised or active during the pilot.** Any real invoicing happens outside the
Platform's automated billing pathway.

## 4. What the Platform is, and is not

The Platform is a workflow and record-keeping tool. During this pilot:

- AML/identity verification, e-signature, payment processing, and live property-data lookups
  operate in test/sandbox mode only, with no real-world legal or financial effect (see the AML/CTF
  Responsibility Statement and Terms of Use).
- The Platform does not hold, receive, or transmit trust money.
- The Platform does not perform, and does not claim to perform, any statutory audit or regulatory
  compliance function on the Brokerage's behalf.
- AI-suggested actions require a human user at the Brokerage to explicitly approve them before they
  take effect — the Platform does not act autonomously on the Brokerage's transactions.

## 5. Brokerage responsibilities

The Brokerage remains solely responsible for:

- Its own compliance with Queensland real estate agent licensing requirements, including its
  obligations under the *Property Occupations Act 2014* (Qld) and the *Agents Financial
  Administration Act 2014* (Qld) governing trust accounts — **legislation references to be
  confirmed by a qualified Australian lawyer as currently in force and applicable**.
- Its own AML/CTF obligations, including any obligations that apply, or come to apply, to real
  estate agents — see the separate AML/CTF Responsibility Statement.
- The accuracy of information its staff enter into the Platform, and obtaining any consent needed
  from its clients (buyers, sellers, solicitors) before entering their personal information into a
  pilot system.
- Ensuring its staff who use the Platform have accepted the Pilot Terms of Use.
- Continuing to run its trust account and any statutory record-keeping through its existing,
  outside-the-Platform systems and processes — not through the Platform.

## 6. Provider responsibilities

The Provider will: make the Platform available for the pilot term; keep AML, e-signature, payment,
and property-data integrations in sandbox/test mode unless the parties separately agree in writing
to enable a live integration; maintain the invitation-only authentication approach described in the
Terms of Use and Privacy Policy; and notify the Brokerage of any confirmed security incident or data
breach per the Incident Response and Breach Notification Summary.

## 7. Data handling

Personal information handled through the pilot is subject to the Pilot Privacy Policy and the Data
Retention Schedule. The Brokerage should ensure it has an appropriate basis (e.g., its own privacy
notice to clients, or the consent captured via the Data Collection and AI Processing Consent Notice)
before entering a transaction party's personal information into the Platform.
