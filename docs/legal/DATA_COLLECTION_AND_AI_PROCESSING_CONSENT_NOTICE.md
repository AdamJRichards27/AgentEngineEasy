## 1. What we are asking you to understand and acknowledge

By accepting your invitation and using the Platform during this pilot, you acknowledge that:

1. Your account information (name, email, password hash, role, and login/security metadata) will
   be collected and stored to operate your account, as described in the Pilot Privacy Policy.
2. Any transaction-party personal information you enter into the Platform (buyer, seller, or
   solicitor names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses) will be stored and used to run that
   property transaction, and may be processed by the AI-assisted features described below.
3. Selected AI-assisted features send relevant property/transaction data to an AI processing
   capability to generate a suggestion, draft, or summary. **No AI-suggested action is applied to a
   real record automatically — a human user must explicitly approve it first.** See the AI Use
   Disclosure for full detail.
4. During this pilot, AML/identity verification, e-signature, and payment features run in
   test/sandbox mode only and have no real-world legal or financial effect.
